Pokémon Starter Guide

There are currently over 1,000 known Pokémon species.

The three original Kanto starter Pokémon are:
- Bulbasaur (Grass/Poison)
- Charmander (Fire)
- Squirtle (Water)

Pikachu is the mascot of the Pokémon franchise and is an Electric-type Pokémon.

Legendary Pokémon are usually rare and have significant lore.
Examples include Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres, Mewtwo, Lugia, Rayquaza, Dialga, and Zacian.

Pokémon evolve in different ways:
- Reaching a certain level
- Using evolution stones
- High friendship
- Trading
- Special locations or conditions

The goal of many trainers is to complete the Pokédex and become the Pokémon Champion.
